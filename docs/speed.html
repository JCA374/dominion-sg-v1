Speed

The machine: a 4-core / 8-thread laptop CPU (i5-8250U), 7 GB RAM, no GPU. Everything is measured, not estimated.

Where the time goes

A teacher rollout (one candidate, one hidden-card draw, played to the end with c2 for both players) takes about 1.6 ms on an idle core and about 5 ms when 7 workers share the 4 cores. A buy decision with 6 candidates at 32 draws is about 200 rollouts; with validation, 260. A teacher game costs 6–11 s on one core; a plain-net game 5 ms. Earlier profiling (BV-v1) put 83% of a rollout in the network's forward pass and under 3% in the rules; its sparse forward pass (fastnet) gave 2.2× end to end.
