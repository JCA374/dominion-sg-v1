What earlier projects taught

  • RL-v3: a turn planner at play time gave no gain (48–50%); improvements came from training. The teacher here plays to the end of the game, not one turn.
  • BV-v1: a separate Deep Sets student of the buy teacher stayed at 50% after 480,000 labels: here c2 itself is the student, starting from its own policy.
  • BV-v1: noise overrides cost points (a 16-draw teacher without a gate scored 41.5%); the z-gate and independent validation draws keep only real gains.
